# Phishing Email Analysis

## Sample Email

**Sender:** security@example-test.com  
**Subject:** Urgent: Verify Your Account

### Observation 1 — Urgent Language
The message creates urgency by asking the user to verify the account quickly.

### Observation 2 — Sender
The sender address should always be checked carefully before trusting an email.

### Observation 3 — Personal Information
A legitimate organization should not be trusted automatically when an email asks for sensitive information.

### Observation 4 — Links
Links should be inspected carefully before clicking.

## Conclusion

The sample demonstrates several common phishing warning signs. 
Users should verify the sender and destination before taking action.
